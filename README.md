Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261117` bounty ($250, program/v1): one
image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026.

The image is a dense single front page (`draw_news.py`, Pillow), with a
masthead naming the day, a Gulf lead story beside a world-at-a-glance map
and the Shenzhen summit, three illustrated features, three columns of
short items, a markets strip and an also-in-brief footer — about 27
stories in all, each reported on 17 November 2026:

1. **US jets strike Iran's Qeshm Island** — American jets hit a
   Revolutionary Guard missile site; Iran fires drones at Bahrain and
   Kuwait; a third US carrier arrives in the region.
2. **Trump, Xi and Putin head for Shenzhen** — Trump to meet Xi at
   APEC; a three-way meeting with Putin may follow.
3. **Ukraine and Russia trade bridge strikes** — the Crimea link is
   damaged; Kyiv's North and South bridges close after Russian strikes.
4. **Russian An-12 crashes in Siberia; 19 dead** — a military
   transport goes down near Kemerovo; a technical fault is blamed.
5. **US loses measles-free status** — 5,400+ cases and 10 deaths end
   26 years of elimination, a first since the early 1990s.
6. Short items: judge cancels student stay limits; California sues
   OpenAI; Chase bug delays Zelle; US data centres emit as much CO2 as
   Denmark; Kushner on Ukraine talks; Raye leads Grammy nominations;
   five EU states plan a Rwanda migrant centre; Jordan and Iraq ban
   Telegram; 'double tap' strike kills 72; Japan protests Russian jets;
   Ebola passes 11,300 cases; Le Pen on Ukraine aid; tech stocks plunge
   (Nasdaq −2.49%); Nikkei nears a bear market; Nvidia refuses Huang's
   return; Brent $91.56; US chip curbs hit Asia's data centres; France
   strikes — plus Tuesday's market closes (S&P 500 −1.92%, gold
   $3,879.60, Bitcoin $64,591).

Sources: Reuters, AP, BBC, UN News, Le Monde, ECDC.

The program (`main.ts`) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The file is a PNG under 1 MiB, 1024 by 1024, within the gate's limits.
