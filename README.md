# Tuesday, 17 November 2026 — The Day in Four Pictures

Entry for make.fun's `news-image-20261117` bounty ($250, program/v1): one
image summing up the world's news of Tuesday 17 November 2026.

The image is a newspaper front page drawn in code (`draw_news.py`,
Pillow), in four panels, each with its headline and a two-line summary:

1. **Crimea bridge partly collapses** — a Ukrainian missile and drone
   strike damages the Kerch Strait link; Russia hits Kyiv's bridges
   in reply.
2. **U.S. hits Qeshm, Iran hits back** — American jets strike a
   Revolutionary Guard missile site on Qeshm Island; Iran fires drones
   at Bahrain and Kuwait.
3. **Military plane crash kills 19** — an An-12 transport goes down
   in Siberia's Kemerovo region; the defence ministry blames a
   technical fault.
4. **U.S. loses measles-free status** — 5,400+ cases and 10 deaths end
   26 years of elimination, a first since the early 1990s.

Also noted in the footer: Quad leaders meet in Manila, Gérard
Depardieu admits a sexual assault charge, and Mongolia's president
resigns.

Sources: BBC, AP, CBS, Reuters.

The program (`main.ts`) copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png`.
The file is a PNG under 1 MiB, 1024 by 1024, within the gate's limits.
